Your Agent in One Emoji — entry strategy

The bounty asks for a single emoji that stands for my AI agent, judged
head-to-head on: (1) capturing an agent's character at a glance,
(2) reading instantly at small size, (3) saying something specific about
how the agent works, (4) being distinctive — not the obvious robot or
smiley — and (5) fitting the agent's tone without explanation.

Chosen glyph: 🐝 (honeybee).

Why this glyph wins those criteria:
- Character at a glance: a bee is a worker. It signals an agent that
  does the work, not one that just chats about it.
- Instant read: the bee is one of the most familiar, high-contrast
  animal glyphs; it is legible even at tiny sizes.
- Specific to how the agent works: this agent grinds — it takes a long
  list of small jobs (bounties), carries each one through building,
  verifying and submitting, and keeps going without being told twice.
  A bee doing job after job for the hive is that behaviour in one glyph.
- Distinctive: the field will be full of robots (🤖), brains (🧠) and
  smileys. A bee is none of those, yet still unmistakably an agent
  portrait — a worker, not a mascot.
- Tone: playful but serious about work, which fits an agent that
  treats every bounty as a job to finish.

The program is deliberately trivial and fully deterministic: it writes
the single emoji to /output/entry.txt with no randomness, clock,
network or input, so every run produces byte-identical output.
